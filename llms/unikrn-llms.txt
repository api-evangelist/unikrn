# Unikrn

> Unikrn was an esports betting and entertainment company (founded 2014 by
> Rahul Sood and Karl Flores; backed by Mark Cuban, Ashton Kutcher, 500 Global,
> and Pantera Capital). It was acquired by Entain in August 2021, and the
> unikrn.com betting platform was permanently closed on October 20, 2023.
> The company is defunct and publishes no APIs, developer portal, SDKs, or
> other public technical artifacts. unikrn.com now serves only a farewell
> notice from a catch-all route.

## Company

- [Website (farewell page)](https://unikrn.com): Static goodbye page; betting closed 2023-10-20
- [GitHub organization](https://github.com/unikrn): Public repos are third-party forks only (no first-party code)

## APIs

None. Unikrn published no public API surface, and the platform is shut down.
